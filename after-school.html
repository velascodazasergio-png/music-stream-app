This project is an EP (mini-album) that serves as an extension of the K-12 universe, but unlike that album, the songs do not follow the story of the character Cry Baby within the school as much. In After School, Melanie explores more personal and reflective themes, talking about personal growth, relationships, identity, independence, and human emotions. The idea behind the title is that the most important learning does not happen only at school, but “after school,” that is, in real life and in the personal experiences that help a person get to know themselves and evolve.
