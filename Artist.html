Melanie Martinez

Alt Pop

Melanie Martinez is an American singer, songwriter, and director known for her dark, whimsical aesthetic and concept albums. Her music blends dreamy pop with unsettling themes, creating a unique universe of characters and stories.

Albums
Cry Baby (2015) K-12 (2019) After School EP (2020) Portals (2023)

Evanescence

Rock / Metal

Evanescence is an American rock band formed in Little Rock, Arkansas in 1995. Led by vocalist Amy Lee, they blend alternative metal, gothic rock, and classical influences into a powerful, emotionally charged sound.

Albums
Fallen (2003) The Open Door (2006) Evanescence (2011) The Bitter Truth (2021)

Mitski

Indie Rock

Mitski Miyawaki is a Japanese-American singer-songwriter known for her emotionally raw and intense music. Her work explores themes of loneliness, identity, and longing with a sound that shifts between intimate folk and explosive indie rock.

Albums
Bury Me at Makeout Creek (2014) Puberty 2 (2016) Be the Cowboy (2018) Laurel Hell (2022)

Billie Eilish

Pop / Alt

Billie Eilish is an American singer and songwriter who first gained widespread recognition at age 13. Known for her whispery vocals, genre-blending sound, and bold visual identity, she became the youngest artist to win the four major Grammy categories in the same year.

Albums
When We All Fall Asleep (2019) Happier Than Ever (2021) Hit Me Hard and Soft (2024)

Ariana Grande

Pop / R&B

Ariana Grande is an American singer, songwriter, and actress from Boca Raton, Florida. Known for her four-octave vocal range, she became one of the best-selling music artists of all time. Her music blends pop, R&B, and trap with deeply personal storytelling.

Albums
Yours Truly (2013) Sweetener (2018) Thank U, Next (2019) Positions (2020)

Rihanna

Pop / R&B

Robyn Rihanna Fenty is a Barbadian singer, actress, and businesswoman. One of the best-selling music artists of all time with over 250 million records sold worldwide, known for her versatile vocal style and ability to adapt to multiple musical genres.

Albums
Music of the Sun (2005) Good Girl Gone Bad (2007) Rated R (2009) Anti (2016)

Lady Gaga

Pop / Dance

Lady Gaga is an American singer, songwriter, and actress known for her musical versatility, visual experimentation, and provocative work. She has sold more than 170 million records worldwide and won numerous Grammy Awards throughout her career.

Albums
The Fame (2008) The Fame Monster (2009) Born This Way (2011) Chromatica (2020)

TV Girl

Indie Pop

TV Girl is an American indie pop band from San Diego, California. Known for their dreamy, sample-based sound that blends lo-fi indie pop with nostalgic references to pop culture. Their music features bittersweet melodies and witty lyrics about modern romance.

Albums
French Exit (2014) Who Really Cares (2014) Death of a Party Girl (2023)
