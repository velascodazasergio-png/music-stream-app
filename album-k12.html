Melanie Martinez's album K-12 is a conceptual album that follows the story of the character Cry Baby within a fictional school. Through its songs and the movie that accompanies the album, the educational system, social norms, and the injustices faced by young people, such as bullying, social pressure, gender stereotypes, and authority control, are criticized. Using a childlike aesthetic but with deep themes, the album shows how Cry Baby and her friends discover the reality of the world, question the rules, and seek to free themselves from a system that tries to shape them.
