It deals with fears and the dark side of fame. Each song represents a different “monster” or fear related to love, fame, relationships, and public life, such as the fear of love, sex, loneliness, or death. Although it maintains the glamorous aesthetic of the album The Fame, this project shows a darker and more emotional view of fame and its consequences.
